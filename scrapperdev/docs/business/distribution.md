# Distribution Map — No-Paid-Ads Channels by Vertical

All channels ranked by **reachability** (how fast you can get in front of decision-makers) and
**cost** (time + money). "Cost" here means your effort, not ad spend.

## Cross-Vertical Rule

**Never lead with the product. Lead with the template pack.**
"Acá están las 20 plantillas de WhatsApp para abogados (gratis)" outperforms "Probá nuestra
extensión" in every channel. The download/install is the secondary CTA, not the first.
